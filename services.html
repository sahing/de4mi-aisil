Enterprise FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from CIOs, procurement heads, and technology leaders regarding our engagement model.

How do you guarantee data privacy when deploying enterprise AI?

We deploy sovereign private landing zones within your cloud boundaries (AWS, Azure, GCP). Your proprietary enterprise data is never used to train public foundation models, and all interactions comply with ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and India's DPDP Act.

Can we engage Alisil for a specific service line rather than the full portfolio?

Yes. While our five pillars are designed for cohesive cross-functional transformation, over 60% of our clients begin with a targeted high-impact engagement—such as Cloud FinOps, Legacy Code Modernization, or BPO Contact Center Copilots—and scale organically.

What SLA guarantees do you provide for critical business operations?

We offer up to 99.999% SLA availability for core cloud transit links, sub-minute MTTR for cognitive incident resolution, and financially backed SLAs on transactional accuracy across BPO and KPO workflows.
